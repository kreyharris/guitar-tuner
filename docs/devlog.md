**September**

I was inspired to work on something music related after my circuits professor brought in a 

distortion guitar pedal he made from scratch on a breadboard. Starting out, however, was quite

overwhelming as we hadn't even reached capacitors in our class yet. I spent hours learning about

the components needed for a simple tuner and ordered them online, learning more and drafting

as they were being delivered.



**Oct 4, 2026**

The most challenging part of this project so far has been 1) understanding op amps, and 2) working

with the Arduino. I'm watching videos on negative feedback loops to understand why we send output

back into the op amp. The Arduino has useful libraries for what I'm trying to do, but I'm more curious

about what is happening under the hood when I use those libraries. In my research, I found that

zero-crossing would likely be the most appropriate way to handle the tuning considering my experience.

The next steps for me are going to be testing gain and probably adding some LED lights to show tune.

I modelled gain in LTspice, but I assume that my physical tuner will behave differently and will need

some adjusting. I have no idea how I'm going to handle tuning with different frequencies or signal processing

in general, but someone on YouTube probably does and that's what matters.
